Designing Readable Web Pages

Good typography and balanced color choices turn raw HTML text into an inviting, easy-to-scan reading experience. When spacing is too tight or contrast is too low, visitors get eye strain and leave early.

Typography and Line Spacing

Setting body text to 16px with a line-height of 1.6 gives each line enough breathing room so the eye can track smoothly from one line to the next without jumping lines.

  • Use a clean sans-serif font family for body copy on screens.
  • Keep line lengths comfortable rather than stretching across wide monitors.
  • Maintain strong contrast between text and background colors.
